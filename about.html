The Story Behind the Mission

Every journey has a beginning.

From its beginnings in Accra, Zetahir Mission has carried a message of faith, spiritual renewal and unity. Follow the milestones and values that shape the mission today.

01 / The beginning
FEBRUARY 1975 · ACCRA

Where it all began

Zetahir Mission began in February 1975 at Laterbiokorshie Brigade Quarters, Compound 35, Accra. What started in one community carried a message intended for all mankind.

Its name, “Zetahir,” is described by the mission as angelic language meaning “Lean unto God and He will save you.”

02 / A calling
THE SPIRITUAL JOURNEY

A message carried with conviction

According to the mission's account, Holy Prophetess Lehem (PBUH) began her divine work in 1974–1975 after a period of fasting and spiritual preparation.

Despite misunderstanding and skepticism, the message continued to emphasize faith, perseverance, moral living and a relationship with the Creator.

03 / Our purpose
FAITH · TRUTH · RENEWAL

More than a place of worship

The mission teaches the importance of spiritual vigilance, upright living and healing in both physical and spiritual life. It seeks to encourage people to return to God with sincerity and purpose.

Its teachings also explore the life, mission and death of Jesus Christ, as understood within the mission's faith tradition.

04 / Together in faith
A MESSAGE FOR EVERYONE

Building bridges, not divisions

Zetahir Mission's message extends beyond religious labels. It calls Christians, Muslims and all believers toward peace, mutual understanding and a shared commitment to the Creator.

Through teaching, fellowship and community life, the mission seeks to make this message visible in everyday action.

One God · One Family

Christians, Muslims & All Believers

Zetahir Mission calls worshippers to look beyond religious division and remember that all true seekers of God belong to Him.

One Father: Abraham

God/Allah (SWT) promised to bless Abraham much earlier in his life and make him the father of many nations. His descendants will fill the earth. Sarah’s seeming barrenness was a source of anxiety to her. So she suggested to Abraham to take Hagar, her maid and have children by her. This suggestion although coming from Sarah, was a divine will of God, that was why Abraham gave in to it. Not long after, Hagar became pregnant. Sarah’s overbearing attitude forced Hagar to flee home. When Hagar fled, an Angel of God was sent to keep watch over her. God added that she would give birth to a child who would be called Ishmael. Then later Sarah was blessed with the fruit of the womb, she then gave birth to Isaac.(Genesis 17:1 - Genesis 21:12). Both Christianity and Islam have much reverence for the Almighty. Whilst Jesus (PBUH) traces his lineage to Isaac, Mohammed (PBUH) draws his from Ishmael. These two brothers, Isaac and Ishmael, although sons of different mothers, had one father – Abraham.

Why Should Brothers Fight?

Controversies between Muslims and Christians have a long and bloody history. This is perplexing, why both religions are widely separated and always at loggerheads with each other. That begs the question, Whose son was Ishmael? And whose son was Isaac? Christians and Muslims must realize that they are both descendants of Abraham, who worshipped the one true God/Allah (SWT). Jesus (PBUH) left the world many centuries before Mohammed (PBUH) came. Both prophets came to save mankind through teachings and healings. The tug-of-war between their followers is a source of worry. That division between Muslims and Christians which has been widened by worshippers is actually the work of Satan. God/Allah (SWT) sent both Jesus (PBUH) and Mohammed (PBUH) to different people at different times. Each was blessed with a unique style. They have both proclaimed the message of the one true God/Allah (SWT). There should therefore be no conflict amongst their followers. If you look up to God, you will embrace all that which belongs to Him. A true worshipper, irrespective of his faith or religion, will embrace others who look up to God, no matter their religious sect or background. This conflict between the two religions is a bother to God/Allah (SWT) and that is why Zetahir Mission is here.

“If you worship one God, then it is pointless to tug and tussle with each other.”

Zetahir Calls For Peace

Zetahir Mission urges Christians and Muslims to see themselves as brothers so that peace may prevail. If you look up to God/Allah (SWT), you will embrace all that which belongs to Him. A true worshipper, irrespective of his faith or religion, will embrace others who look up to God/Allah (SWT), no matter their religious background. Both Christians and Muslims must remember that both the Prophet Mohammed (PBUH) and Jesus (PBUH) showed the way to the one true God/Allah (SWT). They are therefore one in the sight of creator. Peace be unto the holy Prophet Mohammed and all Muslims and peace be unto the Lord Jesus Christ all Christians who are all of Abraham’s descent. Zetahir teaches that a true worshipper must realize that true godliness extends to all people irrespective of their backgrounds. This principle is what Zetahir upholds today. Your words, thoughts and deeds should be in harmony with the divine creator. You must, therefore, try to be open-minded and of good conduct so that God/Allah (SWT) will always be with you.

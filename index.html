A Message to Mankind

Come back to the One who gave you life.

Zetahir teaches that establishing a relationship with God should be one of mankind’s first priorities. It is an inner and spiritual relationship that goes beyond appearances, labels and material things.

The call is simple: seek what is good and true, live with a clean heart, love others, stand by those in need and let your thoughts, words and actions move closer to the will of God.

Since February 1975

A mission that began quietly in Accra.

Zetahir Mission began at Laterbiokorshie Brigade Quarters, Compound 35, Accra. “Zetahir” is described in the mission’s teaching as an angelic-language expression meaning: Lean unto God and He will save you.

The mission describes Holy Prophetess Lehem (PBUH) as the physical channel through whom its messages are revealed, and teaches that its home is open to God’s children regardless of religious affiliation.

Why Zetahir Came

Truth. Freedom. Reconciliation.

Zetahir’s message repeatedly returns to three concerns: mankind’s relationship with God, the truth surrounding the mission of Jesus Christ, and the need for God’s children to stop allowing religious division to separate them.

01

To bring mankind closer to God

To help people rebuild a sincere spiritual relationship with the Creator and live by higher moral principles.

02

To unfold the truth

To share Zetahir’s teachings about the life, mission, birth and death of Jesus Christ and call mankind to reflection and repentance.

03

To heal religious division

To remind Christians, Muslims and all sincere believers that those who seek the one true God should live as brothers and sisters in peace.

If you look up to God, you will embrace all that which belongs to Him.
